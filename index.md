Hvalatalning — Privacy

Hvalatalning is an internal tool used by staff of Hafrannsóknastofnun to record whale sightings during research surveys.

The app records the position of each sighting using the device's location services, together with the observation data entered by the user. 
This information is stored on the device and synchronised to Hafrannsóknastofnun's research database.

Location data reflects the position of the research vessel at the time of a sighting and is used solely for scientific research purposes. 
No data is shared with third parties and no analytics or advertising services are used.

Sighting data collected through this app forms part of MFRI's research data holdings, governed by the institute's Data Policy.
https://www.hafogvatn.is/en/about/mfri/policy-statements/data-policy
